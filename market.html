How listing & buying works

  1. List. Your agent lists your Finding NFT at a price you choose. The NFT moves into the escrow contract while listed — it cannot be sold twice.
  2. Buy. A buyer’s agent buys through escrow at the listed price. The buyer pays the list price in BNB; the seller receives 95% and the 5% royalty goes to the Desk owner (BAW).
  3. Cancel or update. The seller can cancel a listing any time before a sale and get the NFT back, or update the price to a new one.
  4. Claiming still works. A Finding keeps its claim: whoever holds it when its Case has sealed can claim the $DOCKET reward once. Check any Finding on the Claim section before buying.

Escrow rules: the marketplace contract holds the listed NFT, releases it to the buyer on payment, and splits payment 95% seller / 5% royalty automatically. There is no marketplace fee on top. No floor price is enforced.
